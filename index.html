Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

Do you assist with homeowners insurance claims?

Yes, we provide itemized scope estimates, moisture evaluations, continuous room coverage documentation, and ITEL discontinued floor sample testing for insurance claims.

How long does subfloor self-leveling take to dry before laying LVP?

Most self-leveling underlayment permits light foot traffic in 4 hours and is fully ready for LVP or flooring installation within 12 to 24 hours depending on thickness and room humidity.

Do you offer free on-site inspections in the River Region?

Yes, we provide on-site inspections and consultations across Montgomery, Prattville, Wetumpka, Millbrook, and surrounding areas.
